<h1 align="center">cloud3</h1>

<p align="center">
cloud3 is an Android app for watching movies and series.<br>
Add extensions to connect your favorite sources, then browse, search, and save titles to your library.<br>
It also supports downloads, watch history, and different color themes.
</p>
